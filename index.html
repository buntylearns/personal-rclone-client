Personal rclone client

This is a private Google OAuth client used by one person, its owner, with rclone, the open-source file copy tool.

It reads files from a single folder in the owner's own Google Drive, with read-only access, and copies them to the owner's own computer. It can't change, upload or delete anything in Google Drive.

It isn't offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
