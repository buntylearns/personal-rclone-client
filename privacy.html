Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

Who uses it

This Google OAuth client is used only by its owner, with rclone on the owner's own computer. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the read-only Google Drive scope (drive.readonly), rclone lists and downloads files from one folder in the owner's own Google Drive. It doesn't create, change or delete anything in Google Drive.

Where the data goes

The files are copied only to the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to the author of this page, to rclone's developers or to anyone else. There are no analytics, cookies, advertising or sharing.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account, under Security, Your connections to third-party apps and services.
